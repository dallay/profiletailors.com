# Breach Notification — Data Subject Template

> **GDPR Art. 34** — Communication to data subjects when the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

## Overview

This template is used when a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to data subjects' rights and freedoms, requiring direct communication under GDPR Art. 34. Customise per incident and communicate without undue delay using clear and plain language.

## Usage

### Subject Line

**Security Incident Notification — [INCIDENT REFERENCE]**

### Body

Dear [DATA SUBJECT / User],

We are writing to inform you of a security incident that affects your personal data.

#### What happened

[Describe the nature of the breach in plain language. Avoid technical jargon.]

#### What personal data was involved

[List the categories of personal data affected by this incident.]

#### What we have done

[Describe the measures taken to contain and remediate the incident.]

#### What you can do

- If your password was affected, change it immediately on any service where you use the same password.
- Be vigilant for suspicious emails or messages requesting personal information.
- [Additional recommended actions specific to the incident.]

#### Further information

If you have questions, please contact our privacy team at [PRIVACY EMAIL].

We take the security of your data seriously and apologise for any concern this incident may cause.

**Sincerely,**

The Profile Tailors Security Team

## Troubleshooting

- **Language requirements:** Customise the plain-language descriptions to match the data subject's language and locale. For EEA residents, the notification must be in the language of the data subject's member state.
- **High risk determination:** If unsure whether notification is required, err on the side of notifying. Consult the [incident runbook](incident-response-runbook.md) and document the risk assessment rationale.
