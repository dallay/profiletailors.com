This Acceptable Use Policy describes rules and prohibited activities for using Profile Tailors.

## 1. Prohibited Conduct and Content

You may not use Profile Tailors to engage in any of the following activities. This list applies to all content you create, upload, schedule, or publish through the platform, as well as to your general use of the service:

**Illegal Activity:** Any use that violates applicable law or regulation.

**Abuse and Harassment:** Posting content that harasses, threatens, bullies, or intimidates others; inciting violence; or promoting self-harm.

**Hateful Content:** Posting content that promotes or incites hatred, discrimination, or violence based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

**Exploitation:** Any content involving the exploitation of children, including child sexual abuse material. We report all detected instances to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant authorities.

**Non-Consensual Intimate Content:** Posting intimate or sexually explicit content without the consent of all identifiable individuals.

**Fraud and Impersonation:** Impersonating any person or organisation, or engaging in fraud, phishing, or deceptive practices.

**Malware and Unauthorised Access:** Uploading viruses, malware, or other malicious code; attempting to access systems, accounts, or data without authorisation.

**Spam:** Sending unsolicited bulk messages, promotional content, or repetitive content.

**Infringement:** Posting content that infringes on the intellectual property, privacy, or other rights of others.

**Platform Policy Violation:** Using the platform in a way that violates the terms of a connected social platform (such as LinkedIn).

## 2. Enforcement

When we become aware of a potential violation, we review the evidence and take proportionate action:

**Assessment:** We evaluate the severity, recurrence, and impact of the violation, as well as any legal obligations or emergency circumstances.

**Actions May Include:** Content removal, feature restriction (e.g., disabling publishing), temporary suspension, or account termination.

**Notice:** We will provide notice to the affected account holder explaining the action, the reason, and any steps required to remedy the violation, unless providing notice would compromise an investigation or violate legal requirements.

**Appeal:** Account holders may appeal enforcement actions by contacting contact@profiletailors.com. Appeals are reviewed by a human within 14 days.

**Reinstatement:** Where a violation is remedied and no further risk exists, we may reinstate access or content at our discretion.

**Reporting to Authorities:** We may report violations to law enforcement or regulatory authorities when required by law or when we reasonably believe there is a risk of serious harm.

## 3. Reporting Violations

To report a violation of this policy, contact us at contact@profiletailors.com. Please include:

- The specific content or behaviour you are reporting.
- The location of the content (URL, account name, publication date).
- The reason you believe it violates this policy.
- Your contact information (optional, but helpful for follow-up).

We acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and aim to resolve reports within 14 days. Reports may be escalated to qualified legal counsel or law enforcement as appropriate. We keep reports confidential to the extent permitted by law. Knowingly submitting a false report may result in action against the reporter.
