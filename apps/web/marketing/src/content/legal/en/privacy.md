Dallay LLC (Wyoming, United States) operates Profile Tailors, a social content planning and publishing platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you visit our marketing site, register for early access, or use the dashboard and its features. We act as a controller for account, security, and operational data, and as a processor when handling social content you direct through our platform.

Dallay LLC

Registered address: 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States

Contact: contact@profiletailors.com

Profile Tailors is a product name of Dallay LLC. For data-protection inquiries, contact us at contact@profiletailors.com. We do not have a dedicated Data Protection Officer; privacy matters are handled by our operations team and escalated to qualified legal counsel as needed.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal data:

**Account Data:** email address, password (stored as a salted hash), account creation timestamp, and authentication history.

**Profile Data:** display name, avatar image, preferred language, and theme preference.

**Workspace Data:** workspace name, member email addresses, membership roles, and activity logs.

**Social Platform Data:** LinkedIn connection credentials (encrypted OAuth tokens), LinkedIn profile identifiers, and publication content and delivery status.

**Publication Data:** drafted, scheduled, and published content, including text, media uploads, and delivery timestamps.

**Waitlist Data:** email address and consent records (early-access interest and marketing preference).

**Technical Data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, and page interaction timestamps.

**Cookie and Storage Data:** as described in our Cookie Policy and section 8 of this notice.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data for the following purposes:

**Service Delivery:** creating and managing accounts, authenticating users, processing publication scheduling and delivery, and maintaining workspaces.

**Security and Integrity:** detecting and preventing unauthorised access, fraud, and abuse; maintaining session security; and auditing access to sensitive operations.

**Communication:** sending transactional emails (account verification, password reset, delivery notifications) and, with separate consent, marketing communications about product updates and features.

**Analytics:** measuring aggregate usage of the marketing site and dashboard to improve functionality and user experience.

**Legal Compliance:** fulfilling data-subject rights requests, maintaining consent records, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Where we process data as a processor on behalf of a workspace customer, that customer determines the purpose and legal basis. Where we act as a controller, our legal bases include contract necessity (account creation and service delivery), consent (marketing communications and non-essential cookies), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, and product improvement).

## 4. Data Sharing and Recipients

We share personal data only with providers that support the operation of Profile Tailors:

**LinkedIn:** when you connect a LinkedIn account, we transfer publication content and receive delivery status and profile identifiers. LinkedIn processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

**Resend (conditional):** transactional email delivery (account verification, password reset, delivery notifications) is handled through Resend when configured. Resend receives recipient email addresses and message content.

**Ahrefs Web Analytics (conditional):** when enabled, Ahrefs receives anonymised, cookieless page-view data from the marketing site. No personal identifiers are shared.

**Hosting and Infrastructure:** our application code, database, and media storage are deployed on infrastructure provided by our hosting provider. Data processed includes all of the categories listed in section 2.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers. Any new provider is added only after contractual and security review.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy:

**Account Data:** retained until the account is deleted or inactive for 24 consecutive months.

**Workspace Data:** retained until the workspace is deleted or the membership is removed.

**Social Platform Credentials:** encrypted OAuth tokens are retained until the connection is revoked or the account is deleted.

**Publication Data:** retained until the publication is deleted by the user or upon account deletion.

**Media Assets:** retained until the associated publication is deleted; orphaned media is removed after a seven-day reconciliation period.

**Waitlist Data:** retained until early access opens or the individual requests removal.

**Technical Logs:** retained for 90 days, then anonymised or deleted.

**Consent Records:** retained for the duration of the processing activity plus any applicable statutory limitation period.

Legal holds, ongoing investigations, or regulatory requirements may override standard retention periods.

## 6. International Transfers

Dallay LLC is based in the United States. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and in other countries where our providers operate. When transferring personal data from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions with transfer restrictions, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission or other recognised transfer mechanisms. You may request a copy of the applicable safeguards by contacting us at contact@profiletailors.com.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

**Access:** request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

**Correction:** request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

**Deletion:** request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.

**Restriction:** request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances.

**Objection:** object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.

**Portability:** request a structured, machine-readable copy of data you provided.

**Withdraw Consent:** withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@profiletailors.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Cookies and Browser Storage

We use cookies and browser storage as described in our separate [Cookie Policy](/cookies/). In summary:

**Essential Storage:** an HttpOnly session refresh cookie (pt_refresh), a JavaScript-accessible sidebar preference cookie (sidebar_state), and local storage for theme, locale, workspace identifiers, and dashboard state.

**Analytics:** Ahrefs Web Analytics is conditionally enabled on the marketing site and operates without cookies or persistent identifiers by default.

No non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are enabled without your prior consent where legally required.
