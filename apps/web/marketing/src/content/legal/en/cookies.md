## 1. What Are Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. Browser storage (local storage, session storage) serves a similar purpose but is managed directly by the website's code rather than through HTTP headers. This policy describes the cookies and browser storage used by the Profile Tailors marketing site and dashboard, their purpose, and your choices.

## 2. Strictly Necessary Storage

These technologies are required for the platform to function and cannot be disabled:

**pt_refresh** — HttpOnly cookie that stores a refresh-session token. Secure by default, SameSite=Lax, path=/api/auth. Maximum age: 7 days. Cleared on logout.

**sidebar_state** — JavaScript-accessible cookie that stores the dashboard sidebar collapse state. Maximum age: 7 days.

**Local storage (marketing site)** — stores theme preference (light or dark). No expiry; cleared when the user clears browser data.

**Local storage (dashboard)** — stores locale preference, theme setting, active-workspace identifier and name, dashboard view state, and publication draft content. These entries have no automatic expiry; they are cleared on logout or when the user clears browser data.

## 3. Analytics and Similar Technologies

**Ahrefs Web Analytics** is conditionally enabled on the marketing site. When configured, it operates without cookies and without persistent identifiers by default. It collects anonymised page-view data (referrer, browser type, approximate location at country level). No personal data is collected through this technology, and no advertising or behavioural tracking is performed.

## 4. Third-Party Contexts

When you click a link to an external platform (such as LinkedIn) or interact with that platform through our service, the external platform may set its own cookies or use its own storage. These are controlled by the third party's own privacy and cookie policies, not by Profile Tailors.

Our hosting infrastructure provider may set security-related headers and caching mechanisms, but does not set cookies for tracking or analytics purposes on our platform.

## 5. Your Choices

**Essential Storage** cannot be disabled through our platform because it is required for the service to function. You can clear essential cookies and local storage through your browser settings, but this may affect platform functionality.

**Non-Essential Technologies** remain disabled until you provide consent where legally required. Currently, no non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are active by default. If we introduce consent-requiring technologies in the future, we will provide a preference mechanism.

**Browser Settings:** Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. However, disabling all cookies may prevent the platform from functioning correctly.

**Local Storage Deletion:** You can clear local storage through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings. This will reset theme preferences, sidebar state, and unsaved draft content.
